Where Global Capital Meets Indian Innovation.

Ovington Capital Partners is a Luxembourg-domiciled investment and advisory platform founded by a multidisciplinary team combining investment, governance, venture building, and cross-border operating experience. We operate at the intersection of European institutional capital and India's world-class innovation economy.

Our investment focus Meet the team
An India-focused investment holding
“Ovington exists to connect European capital and expertise with India-linked, growth-driven opportunities.”

Ovington is an Investment Holding aiming to generate value from India's secular growth, translating deep knowledge around the country's structural expansion and a strong global operational network into selected investments.

A Luxembourg-domiciled holding investing in selected India-listed companies, accessed as a SEBI-registered Foreign Portfolio Investor, alongside India-linked private opportunities from seed to pre-IPO.
